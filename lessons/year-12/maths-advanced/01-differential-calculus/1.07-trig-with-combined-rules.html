I DoEpisode 7 · Trig with combined rules
Differentiate $y=x^{2}\sin x$, giving your answer in factorised form.
$u=x^{2},\ u'=2x \qquad v=\sin x,\ v'=\cos x$
$\dfrac{dy}{dx}=u'v+uv'=2x\sin x+x^{2}\cos x$
$\phantom{\dfrac{dy}{dx}}=x(2\sin x+x\cos x)$

$\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{u'v-uv'}{v^{2}}=\dfrac{x\cos x-\sin x}{x^{2}}$
We Do 4Episode 7 · Trig with combined rules
Differentiate $y=e^{3x}\cos 2x$, giving your answer in factorised form.
$u=e^{3x},\ u'=3e^{3x} \qquad v=\cos 2x,\ v'=-2\sin 2x$
$\dfrac{dy}{dx}=3e^{3x}\cos 2x-2e^{3x}\sin 2x$
$\phantom{\dfrac{dy}{dx}}=e^{3x}(3\cos 2x-2\sin 2x)$
We Do 5Episode 7 · Trig with combined rules
Differentiate $y=\ln(\cos x)$.
$f(x)=\cos x,\ f'(x)=-\sin x$
$\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{f'(x)}{f(x)}=\dfrac{-\sin x}{\cos x}=-\tan x$
We Do 6Episode 7 · Trig with combined rules
Find the equation of the tangent to $y=x\sin x$ at the point where $x=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$.
Point: $y=\dfrac{\pi}{2}\sin\dfrac{\pi}{2}=\dfrac{\pi}{2}$, so $\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2},\,\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$
Gradient: $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\sin x+x\cos x$, so $m=1+\dfrac{\pi}{2}\times 0=1$
$y-\dfrac{\pi}{2}=1\left(x-\dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$
$y=x$
We Do 7Episode 7 · Trig with combined rules
The depth of water at a jetty is $D=2\cos\dfrac{\pi t}{6}+5$ metres, where $t$ is the time in hours after midnight.
a) Find the depth at midnight.
$t=0$: $D=2\cos 0+5=7$ m
b) Find the rate at which the depth is changing at 2 am, to 2 decimal places. Is the water rising or falling?
$\dfrac{dD}{dt}=-2\times\dfrac{\pi}{6}\sin\dfrac{\pi t}{6}=-\dfrac{\pi}{3}\sin\dfrac{\pi t}{6}$
$t=2$: $-\dfrac{\pi}{3}\sin\dfrac{\pi}{3}=-\dfrac{\pi\sqrt{3}}{6}=-0.906\ldots$
Falling at about $0.91$ m per hour.
