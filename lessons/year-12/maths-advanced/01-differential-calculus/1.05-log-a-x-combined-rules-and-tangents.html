FactEpisode 5 · log_a x and combined rules
The derivative of $\log_{a}x$
$\dfrac{d}{dx}\left(\log_{a}x\right)=\dfrac{1}{x\ln a}$
Proof: $\log_{a}x=\dfrac{\ln x}{\ln a}=\dfrac{1}{\ln a}\cdot\ln x$, so $\dfrac{d}{dx}\left(\log_{a}x\right)=\dfrac{1}{\ln a}\cdot\dfrac{1}{x}$.
$\dfrac{1}{\ln a}$ is just a number. Change to $\ln$ first, then differentiate as usual.

Differentiate $y=x^{2}\ln x$, giving your answer in factorised form.
$u=x^{2},\ u'=2x \qquad v=\ln x,\ v'=\dfrac{1}{x}$
$\dfrac{dy}{dx}=2x\ln x+x^{2}\times\dfrac{1}{x}=2x\ln x+x$
$\phantom{\dfrac{dy}{dx}}=x(2\ln x+1)$

We Do 4Episode 5 · log_a x and combined rules
Find the equation of the tangent to $y=\ln x$ at the point where $x=e$.
Point: $y=\ln e=1$, so $(e,\,1)$
Gradient: $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{1}{x}$, so $m=\dfrac{1}{e}$
$y-1=\dfrac{1}{e}(x-e)$
$y=\dfrac{x}{e}$
We Do 5Episode 5 · log_a x and combined rules
Find the equation of the normal to $y=\ln(2x-1)$ at the point where $x=1$. Give your answer in general form.
Point: $y=\ln 1=0$, so $(1,\,0)$
Tangent gradient: $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{2}{2x-1}=2$, so the normal has gradient $-\tfrac{1}{2}$
$y-0=-\tfrac{1}{2}(x-1)$
$x+2y-1=0$
We Do 6Episode 5 · log_a x and combined rules
Find the equation of the tangent to $y=x\ln x$ at the point where $x=e$.
Point: $y=e\ln e=e$, so $(e,\,e)$
Gradient: $\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\ln x+x\times\dfrac{1}{x}=\ln x+1$, so $m=1+1=2$
$y-e=2(x-e)$
$y=2x-e$
We Do 7Episode 5 · log_a x and combined rules
Find the equation of the tangent to $y=\ln(2x)$ that is parallel to the line $y=4x-1$.
$\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{1}{x}$. Parallel means $m=4$, so $\dfrac{1}{x}=4$ and $x=\dfrac{1}{4}$
Point: $y=\ln\dfrac{1}{2}=-\ln 2$, so $\left(\dfrac{1}{4},\,-\ln 2\right)$
$y+\ln 2=4\left(x-\dfrac{1}{4}\right)$
$y=4x-1-\ln 2$
We Do 8Episode 5 · log_a x and combined rules
Show that $\dfrac{d}{dx}[(2x+1)\ln(2x+1)-2x]=2\ln(2x+1)$.
Product rule on $(2x+1)\ln(2x+1)$:
$u=2x+1,\ u'=2 \qquad v=\ln(2x+1),\ v'=\dfrac{2}{2x+1}$
$\dfrac{d}{dx}[(2x+1)\ln(2x+1)]=2\ln(2x+1)+(2x+1)\times\dfrac{2}{2x+1}=2\ln(2x+1)+2$
So $\dfrac{d}{dx}[(2x+1)\ln(2x+1)-2x]=2\ln(2x+1)+2-2=2\ln(2x+1)$
