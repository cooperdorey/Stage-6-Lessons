FactEpisode 1 · Primitives of x^n
Primitive functions
$F(x)$ is a primitive of $f(x)$ if $F'(x)=f(x)$
$x^{3}$, $x^{3}+5$ and $x^{3}-2$ all have derivative $3x^{2}$, so the general primitive of $3x^{2}$ is $x^{3}+C$.
Finding a primitive reverses differentiation. Always add $+C$.
FactEpisode 1 · Primitives of x^n
The primitive of $x^{n}$
$x^{n}\ \longrightarrow\ \dfrac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}+C,\quad n\neq-1$
Proof: $\dfrac{d}{dx}\left(\dfrac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}+C\right)=\dfrac{(n+1)x^{n}}{n+1}=x^{n}$.
So $kx^{n}\longrightarrow\dfrac{kx^{n+1}}{n+1}+C$ and a constant $k\longrightarrow kx+C$.
Add one to the power, then divide by the new power.
FactEpisode 1 · Primitives of x^n
Notation and rules
$\displaystyle\int f(x)\,dx=F(x)+C$
Read as "the integral of $f(x)$ with respect to $x$".
$\displaystyle\int kf(x)\,dx=k\int f(x)\,dx\qquad \int (f(x)+g(x))\,dx=\int f(x)\,dx+\int g(x)\,dx$
Integrate term by term, with one $+C$ at the end.

We Do 2Episode 1 · Primitives of x^n
Find $\displaystyle\int\left(x^{3}-\dfrac{4}{x^{3}}\right)dx$.
$\displaystyle\int(x^{3}-4x^{-3})dx=\dfrac{x^{4}}{4}-\dfrac{4x^{-2}}{-2}+C$
$=\dfrac{x^{4}}{4}+\dfrac{2}{x^{2}}+C$
We Do 3Episode 1 · Primitives of x^n
Find $\displaystyle\int\left(\sqrt{x}+\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x}}\right)dx$.
$\displaystyle\int(x^{\frac{1}{2}}+x^{-\frac{1}{2}})dx=\dfrac{x^{\frac{3}{2}}}{\frac{3}{2}}+\dfrac{x^{\frac{1}{2}}}{\frac{1}{2}}+C$
$=\tfrac{2}{3}x^{\frac{3}{2}}+2\sqrt{x}+C$

We Do 5Episode 1 · Primitives of x^n
Find $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{x^{4}-3x^{2}+2}{x^{2}}\,dx$.
Divide each term by $x^{2}$: $\displaystyle\int(x^{2}-3+2x^{-2})dx$
$=\dfrac{x^{3}}{3}-3x+\dfrac{2x^{-1}}{-1}+C$
$=\dfrac{x^{3}}{3}-3x-\dfrac{2}{x}+C$
We Do 6Episode 1 · Primitives of x^n
Find $\displaystyle\int(\sqrt{x}+2)^{2}\,dx$.
Expand: $(\sqrt{x}+2)^{2}=x+4x^{\frac{1}{2}}+4$
$\displaystyle\int(x+4x^{\frac{1}{2}}+4)dx=\dfrac{x^{2}}{2}+\dfrac{4x^{\frac{3}{2}}}{\frac{3}{2}}+4x+C$
$=\dfrac{x^{2}}{2}+\tfrac{8}{3}x^{\frac{3}{2}}+4x+C$
