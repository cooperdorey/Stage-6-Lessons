We Do 5 · solutionEpisode 1 · Exponential functions · Solutions
Sketch $y = 2^{x} - 8$.
The $-8$ moves $y = 2^{x}$ down 8, so the asymptote is $y = -8$.
$y$-intercept: $y = 2^{0} - 8 = -7$ → $(0, -7)$
$x$-intercept: $2^{x} = 8 = 2^{3}$, so $x = 3$ → $(3, 0)$ same base, match the powers
Range: $y > -8$
We Do 6 · solutionEpisode 1 · Exponential functions · Solutions
Let $f(x) = 2^{x}$. Show that $f(x+1) - f(x) = f(x)$.
$f(x+1) - f(x) = 2^{x+1} - 2^{x}$
$= 2\times 2^{x} - 2^{x}$ index law: $2^{x+1} = 2\times 2^{x}$
$= 2^{x} = f(x)$
