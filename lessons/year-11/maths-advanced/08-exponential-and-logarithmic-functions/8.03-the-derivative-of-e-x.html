Prior knowledge · solutionEpisode 3 · Derivative of e^x · Solutions
1.
a) $y = x^{4} - 3x$
$\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 4x^{3} - 3$ power rule, term by term
b) $y = (2x + 1)^{3}$
$\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 3(2x + 1)^{2} \times 2 = 6(2x + 1)^{2}$ chain rule: times the derivative of the inside
Category · model solutionEpisode 3 · Derivative of e^x · Solutions
Is it its own derivative? Differentiate and compare.
$y = e^{2x}$: $\;y' = 2e^{2x} \neq y$, no
$y = e^{x}$: $\;y' = e^{x}$, yes
$y = 4e^{x}$: $\;y' = 4e^{x}$, yes
$y = -e^{x}$: $\;y' = -e^{x}$, yes
$y = e^{x} + 3$: $\;y' = e^{x} \neq y$, no the $+3$ disappears

We Do 6 · solutionEpisode 3 · Derivative of e^x · Solutions
Differentiate $y = x^{2}e^{x}$.
$u = x^{2}$, $\;v = e^{x}$, $\;u' = 2x$, $\;v' = e^{x}$ set out $u, v, u', v'$
$\dfrac{dy}{dx} = uv' + vu' = x^{2}e^{x} + 2xe^{x}$
$= xe^{x}(x + 2)$ take out the common factor
We Do 7 · solutionEpisode 3 · Derivative of e^x · Solutions
Differentiate $y = \dfrac{e^{x}}{x}$.
$u = e^{x}$, $\;v = x$, $\;u' = e^{x}$, $\;v' = 1$
$\dfrac{dy}{dx} = \dfrac{vu' - uv'}{v^{2}} = \dfrac{xe^{x} - e^{x}}{x^{2}}$
$= \dfrac{e^{x}(x - 1)}{x^{2}}$ take out the common factor
