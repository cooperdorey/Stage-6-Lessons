$P = 2000e^{0.03t}$. When is $P = 5000$?
$5000 = 2000e^{0.03t}$, so $e^{0.03t} = 2.5$.
$0.03t = \ln 2.5$
$t = \dfrac{\ln 2.5}{0.03} = 30.54\ldots$
After about 30.5 years.
We Do 7 · solutionEpisode 7 · Exponential equations · Solutions
Solve $2^{2x} - 6\left(2^{x}\right) + 8 = 0$.
Let $u = 2^{x}$, so $2^{2x} = u^{2}$. quadratic form
$u^{2} - 6u + 8 = 0$
$(u - 2)(u - 4) = 0$, so $u = 2$ or $u = 4$.
$2^{x} = 2$ gives $x = 1$; $\;2^{x} = 4$ gives $x = 2$.
