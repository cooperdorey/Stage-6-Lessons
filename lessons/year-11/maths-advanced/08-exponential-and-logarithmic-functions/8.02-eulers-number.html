Prior knowledge · solutionEpisode 2 · Euler's number · Solutions
1. For $y = 2^{x}$:
a) $x = 0$: $y = 2^{0} = 1$, so the $y$-intercept is $(0, 1)$.
b) $2^{x} > 0$ for every $x$, so the curve never reaches the $x$-axis.
The asymptote is $y = 0$.

Growth or decay? Look at the sign of the power.
$y = e^{x}$: power $+x$, growth
$y = e^{-x}$: power $-x$, decay
$y = e^{-3x}$: power $-3x$, decay
$y = e^{-0.2x}$: power $-0.2x$, decay
$y = e^{0.5x}$: power $+0.5x$, growth

3.
a) $y = e^{4x}$: power $+4x$, growth
b) $y = e^{-0.5x}$: power $-0.5x$, decay
c) $y = \left(\tfrac1e\right)^{x} = e^{-x}$: decay rewrite first: $\tfrac1e = e^{-1}$
d) $y = e^{x/3}$: power $+\tfrac{x}{3}$, growth

Sketch $y = e^{x}$.
Power $+x$, so the graph grows.
$x = 0$: $y = e^{0} = 1$ → $(0, 1)$
$x = 1$: $y = e^{1} = e \approx 2.72$ → $(1, e)$
Asymptote $y = 0$ $e^{x} > 0$
Domain: all real $x$
Range: $y > 0$
Routine · We Do 1 solutionEpisode 2 · Euler's number · Solutions
Sketch $y = e^{-x}$.
Power $-x$, so the graph decays.
$x = 0$: $y = e^{0} = 1$ → $(0, 1)$
$x = -1$: $y = e^{1} = e$ → $(-1, e)$ decay, so use $x = -1$
Asymptote $y = 0$
Domain: all real $x$
Range: $y > 0$

We Do 3 · solutionEpisode 2 · Euler's number · Solutions
Sketch $y = -e^{x}$.
$k = -1 < 0$: $y = e^{x}$ reflected in the $x$-axis, so the curve sits below the axis.
$x = 0$: $y = -1$ → $(0, -1)$
$x = 1$: $y = -e \approx -2.72$ → $(1, -e)$
Asymptote $y = 0$
Range: $y < 0$
We Do 4 · solutionEpisode 2 · Euler's number · Solutions
Sketch $y = e^{x} + 2$.
The $+2$ moves $y = e^{x}$ up 2, so the asymptote is $y = 2$. vertical translation
$x = 0$: $y = e^{0} + 2 = 3$ → $(0, 3)$
$x = 1$: $y = e + 2 \approx 4.72$
Range: $y > 2$
We Do 5 · solutionEpisode 2 · Euler's number · Solutions
Sketch $y = e^{x+1}$.
Choose $x$ to make the power simple.
$x = -1$: $y = e^{0} = 1$ → $(-1, 1)$
$x = 0$: $y = e^{1} = e$ → $(0, e)$
This is $y = e^{x}$ moved 1 unit left.
Asymptote $y = 0$
Range: $y > 0$
