FactEpisode 4 · Logarithms
Definition of a logarithm
$\log_a y = x \iff a^{x} = y$
where $a > 0$, $a \neq 1$ and $y > 0$
A log is an index: $\log_a y$ is the power you raise $a$ to, to get $y$
FactEpisode 4 · Logarithms
Notation
$\log_{10} y$ is written $\log y$
$\log_e y$ is written $\ln y$  (the natural logarithm)

We Do 2 · solutionEpisode 4 · Logarithms · Solutions
Evaluate $\log_3 \dfrac19$.
$\dfrac19 = \dfrac{1}{3^{2}} = 3^{-2}$ a reciprocal is a negative power
$\log_3 \dfrac19 = -2$
We Do 3 · solutionEpisode 4 · Logarithms · Solutions
Evaluate $\log_4 2$.
$2 = \sqrt4 = 4^{1/2}$ a root is a fractional power
$\log_4 2 = \dfrac12$
We Do 4 · solutionEpisode 4 · Logarithms · Solutions
Solve $\log_5 x = 3$.
Rewrite in index form: $x = 5^{3}$. $\log_a y = x \iff a^{x} = y$
$x = 125$
We Do 5 · solutionEpisode 4 · Logarithms · Solutions
Solve $\log_x 64 = 3$.
Rewrite in index form: $x^{3} = 64$.
$x = \sqrt[3]{64} = 4$ the base must be positive
We Do 6 · solutionEpisode 4 · Logarithms · Solutions
Solve $2^{x} = 12$.
Rewrite in log form: $x = \log_2 12$. $a^{x} = b \iff x = \log_a b$
$x = 3.5849\ldots \approx 3.58$ calculator $\log_{\square}\square$ key
Check: $2^{3.58} \approx 12$.
We Do 7 · solutionEpisode 4 · Logarithms · Solutions
Show that $\log_2 5$ lies between $2$ and $3$.
$2^{2} = 4$ and $2^{3} = 8$, so $2^{2} < 5 < 2^{3}$
$\log_2 x$ increases as $x$ increases, so $\log_2 2^{2} < \log_2 5 < \log_2 2^{3}$
$2 < \log_2 5 < 3$
